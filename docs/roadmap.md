# Roadmap

1. **Platform initialization** — API, worker, persistence, web, observability, Compose.
2. **Incident and timeline** — lifecycle and append-only incident events.
3. **Integration Adapter SDK** — auth, retry, timeout, circuit breaker, secret references.
4. **Playbook model** — versions, typed steps, conditions, and rollback definitions.
5. **Workflow engine** — explicit state machine, retries, timeout, cancellation, compensation.
6. **Human approval** — risk-based gates, multiple approvers, and expiration.
7. **Detection and graph integrations** — AI-SOC alert ingest and ThreatGraph enrichment.
8. **RedMind integration** — analysis, proposed actions, and evidence.
9. **Patchtower response** — dry-run, approval, execution, and rollback.
10. **AutoPentest validation** — authorized post-response attack-path verification.
11. **AIShield integration** — robustness assessment for AI-service incidents.
12. **Operations dashboard** — queues, SLA, metrics, timelines, approvals, and audit views.
13. **Integrated demo** — end-to-end mock incident response and report.

Stage 1 is the current milestone. A later milestone begins only after its predecessor has
been implemented, tested, and reviewed.
